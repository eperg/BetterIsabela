Palay & Corn Price Watch

What farmers are paid, and what the markets charge. Know the number before you agree to it.

Loading the latest prices…

Farmgate price — what the farmer is paid

The monthly average price paid to farmers in Isabela, published by the Philippine Statistics Authority. This is the number to check before meeting a buyer.

Market price — what the public markets charge

Current retail prices in the five Isabela public markets monitored by the Department of Agriculture. Prices differ between towns, sometimes by a wide margin.

Where these numbers come from

    These figures are republished from official sources without alteration. They are a reference, not an offer, and they cannot account for grade, moisture content or volume. Always confirm with the buyer or market before deciding.
